# Refund policy — Northwind Coffee Co. (fictional)

Customers can request a full refund within **30 days** of purchase if the coffee beans are unopened.
Opened bags can be refunded at **50%** within 14 days if the customer reports a quality issue with a photo.
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time; the current month is not refunded.
Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 business days.
