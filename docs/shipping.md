# Shipping — Northwind Coffee Co. (fictional)

Orders placed before **2 pm** ship the same business day.
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days and is free for orders over **US$ 40**; otherwise it costs US$ 6.
Express shipping (1–2 business days) costs US$ 15.
We ship to the United States, Canada, Mexico, Peru and Chile. Customs duties are paid by the customer.
